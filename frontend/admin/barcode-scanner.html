How Auto Check-In / Check-Out Works:
  • 1st Scan: Marks student as PRESENT and activates booked seat (Check-In).
  • 2nd Scan: Checks the student out and releases the seat for other students (Check-Out).
  • Plug-and-play USB scanners automatically type the code and press Enter instantly.
Mobile App ("Barcode to PC") Scanning Tips:
  • • App Settings: In the Barcode to PC app, go to Settings -> Barcode Formats and enable Code 39 and Code 128.
  • • Distance & Focus: Hold your phone camera 15–20 cm (6 inches) away from the ID card. If held too close, the camera blurs and cannot read thin lines.
  • • Orientation: Align the camera so the red scanner line cuts straight horizontally across all black bars.
  • • Digital Barcode: Students can also open their Student Portal (check-in.html) on their phone for an instant, high-contrast digital barcode.
